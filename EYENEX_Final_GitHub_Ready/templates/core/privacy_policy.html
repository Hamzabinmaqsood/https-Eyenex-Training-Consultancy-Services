Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and handle information submitted through the EYENEX website.

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, application, or appointment request, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, city, course of interest, preferred appointment details, and messages. For paid appointment requests, you may also provide a payment screenshot. If you request financial aid for an appointment fee, we collect the reason you provide for that request.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, review applications and appointment requests, verify submitted appointment-payment information, assess financial-aid requests, and communicate relevant next steps about our training programs. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Information submitted through this website is stored for admissions, appointment management, communication, and administrative purposes. Access should be limited to authorized {{ site_short_name }} personnel who need the information for those purposes.

Payment Information

The website may ask you to upload a screenshot confirming an appointment-fee payment. Do not upload banking passwords, PINs, one-time passwords, or unrelated sensitive financial information.

Cookies

This website may use basic session cookies required for core functionality, such as form security and confirmation messages. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

Your Requests

You may contact us to request correction of information you submitted or to ask about how your information is being handled.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, email {{ site_email }} or call {{ site_phone }}.
